SUNSET-LIGHT STOP

Ponte dei Tre Archi

No date is assumed. Choose your event date.

Stops & things to notice

    A starting point, not a shot list. Follow an idea, change it, or ignore it. There is no photograph quota, no need to invent a story, and nothing to submit. Use “Explore freely” to hide the prompts.

    Cameras and phones are equally welcome. Sharing photos and buying anything are optional. Keep doorways, working spaces and narrow bridges clear. Ask permission for close portraits; do not look into homes or use memorials as props.

    These are planning routes, not confirmed events. Pins mark approximate outdoor areas. Check access and bridge crossings on a scouting walk. Routes are not guaranteed step-free. Transport, museums and exhibition admission are not included.

    Biennale stops are outside the ticketed exhibition boundary. The historic Arsenale gate and the Campo della Tana exhibition entrance are separate places. Official entrance information ↗

    Photography ideas & references

    The guide has five optional phone-friendly ideas at every photo stop. Use an ordinary phone camera; special lenses, manual mode, a tripod and paid apps are not required. Main Walk: 11 stops. Full Walk: 28 stops.

    Background reading: Aperture: The Photographer’s Playspace, on open-ended prompts and different interpretations; Tom Bol / Nikon: The Composition Triangle, on camera craft, graphic design and experimentation. The guide adapts those broad themes to an optional, beginner-friendly walk.

    Free map, navigation limits & privacy

    The detailed map uses OpenFreeMap with MapLibre and OpenStreetMap data. It does not use a paid Google Maps API. OpenFreeMap’s public service currently has no per-view fee or API key; availability is not guaranteed. A standard OpenStreetMap map is used if the vector map cannot load.

    Place names appear at street-level zoom to keep the overview quiet. Nearby places are community-mapped listings, not extra walk stops, endorsements or a complete business directory. No live ratings, opening hours or walking-time estimates are attached to them. Labels prefer English where available; proper names remain local.

    Google Maps · The 11-stop Main Walk has four walking parts and two separate waterbus journey links, with a change at Ferrovia. The Full 28-Stop Walk has seven walking parts.

    Map © OpenStreetMap contributors / OpenMapTiles / OpenFreeMap. Street routing: FOSSGIS / OSRM. Report a map issue. Solid lines are computed walking paths; dotted lines only show the visit order. Neither guarantees current access.

    Sunset uses SunCalc 1.9.0 for an unobstructed horizon at Tre Archi, with Europe/Rome daylight saving. The meeting suggestion appears only after street routing succeeds and includes editable pause time. It is a planning estimate, not live navigation.

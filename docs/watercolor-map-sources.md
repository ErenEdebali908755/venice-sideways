## Classic dependency and route integrity

The modern renderer keeps its existing local MapLibre chain. Classic separately vendors exact MapLibre GL 5.24.0 and Leaflet adapter 0.1.3 JS/CSS; their licenses, actual SHA-256/SHA-384 hashes and sizes are in [the Classic manifest](../public/classic-vendor/manifest.json). Its loader requires integrity and never retries through an unhashed fallback. Leaflet 1.9.4 still uses its existing SRI-protected CDN chain, so the remaining CDN CSP permissions are deliberate. [MDN explains browser integrity checks](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/Security/Defenses/Subresource_Integrity).

Classic OSRM responses are bounded before parsing and independently checked against the requested stops, order, leg continuity and distances. A rejected response uses the existing clearly labeled schematic/Google Maps fallback. These structural checks do not prove physical walkability. The existing 100 m snap ceiling is retained; new 20 m endpoint and 10 m continuity tolerances account for coordinate rounding and are covered by captured guide geometry and synthetic tests. Physical pedestrian/bridge acceptance remains separate. [OSRM route response contract](https://project-osrm.org/docs/v5.24.0/api/).

The official-timetable label accepts only exact, checked ACTV/AVM HTTPS pages; credentials, unapproved ports, query/redirect candidates and unrelated hosts are rejected. See [ACTV navigation timetables](https://actv.avmspa.it/en/content/orari-servizio-di-navigazione-0) and [the Italian timetable page](https://actv.avmspa.it/it/content/orari-servizio-di-navigazione-0). Invalid snapshot links use the verified navigation-page fallback without fetching arbitrary targets.

## Location and approved reference photographs

Location permission draws a marker without moving the camera. Explicit follow/recenter can request map data for the displayed geographic area. Raw coordinates are not sent to organisers or anonymous measurement; this does not promise that a network map provider sees no location-derived viewport. Physical-device permission, accuracy and battery tests remain separate.

The three owner-authorized monochrome references are public thumb/web derivatives with minimal eight-language alt text and credit, not a private archive catalog. They are marked `referenceOnly` and are not verified stop photographs. The explicit [selection policy](../public/field-guide/temporary-selection.js) can disable the family or revoke named approved assets. An explicit empty, removed or revoked stop assignment never restores the reference fallback. Disabling/revoking the local policy requires a new deployment; it cannot recall already downloaded public derivatives. The private original library and signed/private media URLs are never a fallback source.

The two small garden polygons in `public/field-guide/gardens.json` were extracted on 2026-10-04 from OpenStreetMap ways [174476472 (Giardini Papadopoli)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/174476472) and [4715855 (Parco Savorgnan)](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/4715855). The file contains their original ring coordinates, names and OSM way IDs. It is derived from OpenStreetMap data and is distributed under [ODbL 1.0](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright); the map source supplies visible contributor attribution. Both ways were tagged `leisure=park` at retrieval. OpenFreeMap's 2026-09-27 z14 Venice tile independently included both names as `poi` park features, but omitted their polygons from its `park` vector layer. That is why the prototype uses this small OSM extract for the two highlighted boundaries.

The "Show on map" view points for the two gardens use the corresponding named park POIs in that OpenFreeMap tile. They lie within the OSM boundaries; they are view centers, not separate stop markers.

The inventory is from 2014. Municipal geometry was not copied, because its reuse terms were not established. The map card asks visitors to check current access locally and does not promise entry or exact hours.

Package use follows the user's integration request for these sites; it does not establish a new general reuse licence, architectural approval or permanent editorial photo/place verification. The pack screens and aspect-ratio diagrams are design/test references, not production route geometry or genuine landscape/panorama photographs.
